About Me

Hi, I’m Sara

Welcome to LayersOfJoy! I’m a passionate baker and cake enthusiast dedicated to sharing the sweet joys of baking with the world.

My love affair with baking started at a young age, inspired by my grandmother’s homemade cakes. Over the years, I have honed my skills, experimented with countless recipes, and developed a deep appreciation for the art of cake-making.

LayersOfJoy is more than just a blog; it’s a celebration of all things cake. Here, you’ll discover a treasure trove of delectable recipes, baking ideas, and creative inspiration to elevate your baking game. Each recipe is carefully crafted and tested to bring a little more joy to every table.
