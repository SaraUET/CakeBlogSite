Tiramisu Cake

Ingredients:

  • 1 package yellow cake mix
  • 1/2 cup strong brewed coffee
  • 8-ounce package of cream cheese
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1-ounce semisweet chocolate, grated

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Mix all the ingredients in a bowl until smooth.
  3. Then grease a 9-inch pan with oil and pour the batter into it.
  4. Put the pan in the preheated oven for 25-30 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  5. When the cake is baked, take it out of the oven and allow it to cool down, then take it out of the pan.
  6. In a bowl, beat cream cheese and sugar until smooth. Gradually add heavy cream, and mix until smooth.
  7. Cut the cake layer horizontally into two layers. Brush each with coffee. Then spread the cream cheese mixture. Top with remaining cake layer.
  8. Dust the top of the cake with cocoa powder and sprinkle with grated chocolate. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours before serving.
